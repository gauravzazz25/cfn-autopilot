Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

CFN Autopilot ("the app") is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Copied From Nature. This policy explains how the app handles Google user data.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube Data API: uploading the owner's videos to the owner's channel, and reading the channel's own video list and statistics.
  • YouTube Analytics API: reading aggregate performance (views, average percentage viewed) of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

Statistics are used only to decide which of the owner's already-produced episodes to schedule next. The app does not access any other person's data.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and none is used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which local data is deleted.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
