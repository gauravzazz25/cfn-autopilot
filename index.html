CFN Autopilot

CFN Autopilot is a private, single-user publishing tool for the YouTube channel Copied From Nature, an educational science Shorts channel about technologies engineers copied from nature.

It runs only on the channel owner's computer. It uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own channel, schedules their publish times, and reads that channel's view statistics to decide which episode to publish next. It is not offered to other users.
